Overview Off

Support · Safari on Mac, iPhone, and iPad

Overview Off hides the AI Overview block on Google Search in Safari. An optional Web mode opens Google's plain Web results.

Turn it on — Mac

  1. Open Safari → Settings → Extensions.
  2. Turn on Overview Off.
  3. Choose Allow for google.com.

Turn it on — iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions.
  2. Tap Overview Off and turn it on.
  3. Set google.com to Allow.

AI Overview still shows

Check that the extension is on and allowed for google.com, then search again. Google changes its pages from time to time; when that breaks hiding, an app update fixes it. Write to us and include your search.

Web mode doesn't apply

A change in the app takes effect on your next search, not on a page that's already open.
