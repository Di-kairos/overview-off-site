Privacy Policy

Overview Off for Safari · Effective November 5, 2026

Overview Off doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party code. The app and its Safari extension make no network requests of their own.

What stays on your device

Your two settings — Hide AI Overview and Web mode — are saved on your device so the app and the extension can read them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Google Search pages

The extension runs only on Google Search pages in Safari. It reads the page to find and hide the AI Overview block. Nothing from the page leaves your device.

With Web mode on, the extension adds udm=14 to your Google search address, so Google opens its Web results. Your searches still go to Google as usual, and Google's privacy policy applies to them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
